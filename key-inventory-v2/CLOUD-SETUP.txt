BAND SYNC - SETUP GUIDE (ACCOUNTS, FRIENDS AND AN ORG CHART)
============================================================

What you get
------------
It works like adding friends:
 1. Everyone registers in the app with their email and a password.
 2. Right after registering, the app asks "What part of the band are you?"
    (Worship leader, Vocalist, Guitarist, Bassist, Drummer, Keyboardist, Sound
    tech, Media / Slides, or your own). It is saved on the person's account.
 3. In Settings > Band & sync, a person adds a friend by typing the email the
    friend registered with. The friend gets a request.
 4. When the friend taps Accept, they appear in each other's ORG CHART (a person
    who adds a guitarist sees the guitarist on their chart, and the guitarist
    sees them), and their songs and setlists stay in step, usually within a
    couple of seconds. Friends of friends join the same chart.
The app still works without internet: it keeps a copy on each device and
catches up when the connection comes back.

You do this setup ONCE for the whole band (about 10 minutes). It uses Firebase,
Google's service. The free plan ("Spark") is plenty for a church band.

Part 1 - Make the online place (one person, once)
-------------------------------------------------
1. Go to https://console.firebase.google.com and sign in with a Google account.
2. Click "Create a project" (or "Add project"). Give it a name, for example
   "Key Inventory". You can turn Google Analytics off. Click Create.
3. Turn on sign-in:
   Build > Authentication > Get started > Sign-in method > Email/Password >
   switch "Enable" on > Save.
4. Make the database:
   Build > Firestore Database > Create database > keep "Production mode" >
   choose a location near you > Enable.
5. Set the rules (this decides who can use the database):
   In Firestore Database, open the "Rules" tab. Delete everything there, paste
   the rules below, and click "Publish".

   rules_version = '2';
   service cloud.firestore {
     match /databases/{database}/documents {
       match /users/{uid} {
         allow read, write: if request.auth != null && request.auth.uid == uid;
         match /invites/{from} {
           allow create: if request.auth != null && request.auth.uid == from;
           allow read, delete: if request.auth != null && (request.auth.uid == uid || request.auth.uid == from);
         }
       }
       match /emails/{h} {
         allow get: if request.auth != null;
         allow create, update: if request.auth != null && request.resource.data.uid == request.auth.uid;
       }
       match /workspaces/{band}/{document=**} {
         allow read, write: if request.auth != null;
       }
     }
   }

   (The app also shows these rules, with a Copy button, in Settings.)
   Tip: after publishing, open the "Rules playground" or just try the app with
   two accounts; if something says "refused access", the rules were not
   published or were pasted incompletely.
6. Get the settings block:
   Click the gear next to "Project Overview" > Project settings > scroll to
   "Your apps" > click the web icon  </>  > give it a nickname > Register app.
   Firebase shows a block starting with  const firebaseConfig = { ... };
   Copy that whole block.

Part 2 - Connect the app (same person)  - TWO WAYS, pick one
------------------------------------------------------------
CLEANEST (recommended): build the settings into the app file, once.
  Open index.html in any text editor (Notepad is fine), search for
  BUILT_IN_FIREBASE, and replace   null   with the block's inside, like this:

     const BUILT_IN_FIREBASE = { apiKey: "...", authDomain: "...", projectId: "...", appId: "..." };

  Upload the file again to your website. From then on nobody on your band sees
  any setup: everyone just registers.

OR, without editing the file:
  1. Open the app (your website) > Settings (the gear) > Band & sync.
  2. Tap "Set up band sync", paste the block from step 6, and tap
     "Save and continue".
  3. To bring a friend in, tap "Copy a setup link for a friend" and send it.
     They open the link and only have to create their account.

Part 3 - Build the org chart
----------------------------
1. Register: tap "Create account", type your name, email and a password.
2. Choose your part in the band.
3. In "Add a friend", type the email your friend registered with, tap "Add
   friend". They see "<your name> added you to their org chart" in their
   Settings > Band & sync, with Accept and Not now.
4. After they accept, both of you see each other on the org chart, and your
   songs and setlists are shared. If the friend already has songs on their
   device, the app asks whether to COMBINE them with the band's songs or use
   ONLY the band's. Choose "Combine both" when unsure: nothing is lost.
5. Anyone can add friends. The org chart shows who added whom. You can take out
   (x) the people you added, or cancel a request that has not been accepted.
   "Leave band" stops syncing on your device (your songs stay on it).

Good to know
------------
- Friends are found by exact email only. Nobody can browse a list of people.
- Everyone in a band sees the band's songs and setlists and can change them.
  The newest change wins if two people edit the same song at the same moment;
  your screen is left alone while you are editing a song.
- "Last changed by" is saved on every song and setlist.
- Changing your name or part updates it on everyone's org chart.
- Songs are combined, never doubled: when friends connect, every song from each
  device is added to the other, and a song you both have (the same title, and
  the same artist unless one of you left the artist empty) is kept only once.
  Nothing is lost: the singers and keys, lyrics and details from both copies
  are put together, and any setlist that used the other copy points to the one
  that stays. All songs are saved on each person's own device too, so they are
  still there offline, and if someone leaves the band.
- Songs that came from a friend show a small "Added by <name>" tag in the song
  list, on the song page and on the Songs overview. Songs you added yourself have
  no tag. If two friends both had the same song, it shows the first person's name
  and "+1" (tap or hover it to see the others).
- Someone who is in another band is asked before switching to a new one.
- Restoring a backup while synced replaces the band's songs too. The app warns
  you first.
- Costs: the free Firebase plan has generous daily limits, far above what a
  church band uses. Singer pictures are stored in the database as small images.
- Security note: the band's data is protected by sign-in, and each band lives
  under a long secret id that only members are told. For a small church band
  that is a good fit. It is not designed for secret or paid content.

If something does not work
--------------------------
- "Email sign-in is not switched on": do step 3 of Part 1.
- "The online database refused access": do step 5 of Part 1 and press Publish.
- "Nobody has registered with that email yet": your friend must create their
  account in the app first; check the spelling of the email.
- Status says "Offline": no internet. Keep working; it syncs when you are back.
- Changed projects or settings by mistake: Settings > Band & sync > "Use
  different online settings".
