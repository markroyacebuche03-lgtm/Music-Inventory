BAND SYNC - SETUP GUIDE
=======================

What your band gets
-------------------
A new person only has to REGISTER WITH THEIR EMAIL (and a password). Then they
search for a friend by email and tap "Add friend". The friend sees a floating
card, taps Accept, and from then on you appear in each other's org chart and
your songs and setlists stay in sync (the same song from two people is kept
once, and each song shows who added it).

Everyone except the person who hosts the app does NOTHING else: no settings to
paste, no rules, no codes, no links.

The host does this ONE TIME (about 10 minutes). It uses Firebase, Google's
free service (the "Spark" plan is plenty for a church band).

HOST SETUP (one time)
---------------------
1. Go to https://console.firebase.google.com, sign in with Google, click
   "Create a project", name it (for example "Key Inventory"). You can turn
   Google Analytics off.

2. Turn on sign-in:  Authentication > Get started > Sign-in method >
   Email/Password > switch "Enable" on > Save.

3. Make the database:  Firestore Database (it may be under "Databases &
   Storage") > Create database > keep "Production mode" > pick a location near
   you > Create.

4. Publish the rules. Open the file  firestore.rules  from this folder on your
   computer (Notepad is fine), press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C. In Firebase go to
   Firestore Database > Rules tab, delete what is there, press Ctrl+V, and
   click "Publish".

5. Get your project's settings:  click Settings (gear, left menu) > General >
   scroll to "Your apps" > click the web icon </> > give it any nickname >
   Register app. Firebase shows a block like:

       const firebaseConfig = {
         apiKey: "AIza...",
         authDomain: "your-project.firebaseapp.com",
         projectId: "your-project",
         storageBucket: "your-project.firebasestorage.app",
         messagingSenderId: "1234567890",
         appId: "1:1234567890:web:abcdef"
       };

6. Open  firebase.js  from this folder (Notepad). Replace each PASTE_... value
   with the matching value from the block (keep the quote marks). Save.

7. Upload  index.html  and  firebase.js  TOGETHER to your website (Netlify,
   GitHub Pages, ...). Open the website address (https://...). Done: anybody
   who opens it can register.

OPTIONAL: Firebase Hosting (no firebase.js to fill in at all)
-------------------------------------------------------------
If you host the app on Firebase Hosting, the app finds your project's settings
by itself (steps 5 and 6 are not needed). With the Firebase tools installed,
run "firebase deploy" in this folder: it publishes the website AND the rules
(firebase.json and firestore.rules are included for that).

What your friends see
---------------------
- A floating card appears when someone adds them, with Accept and Decline (the
  small x only hides it for now; the request stays in Settings > Band & sync).
- If they decline, the person who asked gets a cheerful card ("Joel is not ready
  just yet ... keep going!"), not a plain "no".
- When a friend opens Play mode for a setlist, everyone else sees moving sound
  bars and "Joel started playing <setlist>" (with a Join button) at the top of
  the sidebar, in the Setlist list and in Play mode. It disappears when they stop.
- The tutorial (Settings > Tutorial) has animated scenes for registering, adding
  a friend and the org chart. The rule it teaches: let ONE person (usually the
  worship leader) add everyone, so the whole band ends up in ONE org chart.

Good to know
------------
- Songs from friends are combined on every device and saved there too. The
  same song (same title, same artist or no artist written) is kept once, with
  everyone's singers and keys put together.
- Songs show "Added by <name>" when a friend added them.
- Everyone in a band can see and change the band's songs and setlists. The
  newest change wins if two people edit the same song at the same moment.
- Your nickname starts as the first word of your email (anna.lee@... becomes
  Anna). Change it any time in Settings > Band & sync > "You". Use ONE short word
  your friends will recognize (Maria, PastorJo): it shows on the org chart and as
  "Added by" on songs, and long ones are shortened there. You can also choose
  your part in the band (guitarist, drummer, ...) there.
- Friends are found by exact email only; nobody can browse a list of people.
- If you are already in a band with friends and someone else asks you, you can
  accept; if you are on your own, it is instant. If you are in a band with
  other people, the app asks before you switch.
- The settings in firebase.js are not secret passwords. They only point the app
  to your project. Sign-in and the rules protect the data.
- Costs: the free Firebase plan is far above what a church band uses.

If something does not work
--------------------------
- Tap "Check connection" (Settings > Band & sync, under the sign-in form). It
  says in plain words which step fails.
- "No connection to the online service": open the app from your website
  address (https://...), not by double-clicking index.html on your computer
  (sign-in does not work from a file). Then check the values in firebase.js,
  and try mobile data or another browser (Wi-Fi, ad blockers or a VPN can block
  Google).
- "Email sign-in is not switched on": do step 2 again.
- "The online database refused access": do step 4 again and press Publish.
- "Nobody has registered with that email yet": your friend must register first;
  check the spelling.
- The app says sync is "not switched on yet": firebase.js still has its PASTE_
  values, or was not uploaded next to index.html.
